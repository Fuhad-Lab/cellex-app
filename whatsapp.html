Cellex on WhatsApp

Shop, join group buys, and manage your cart — all from WhatsApp

Link your WhatsApp number Open Cellex WhatsApp bot

Bot commands

link <code>
Link your WhatsApp to your Cellex account (get the code here)
cart
View your current cart
add <id>
Add a product to your cart (use the product ID from any product page)
remove <cart_item_id>
Remove an item from your cart
clear
Clear your cart
checkout
Get the checkout URL to complete your purchase
product <id>
Get details about a product
search <query>
Search for products by name, description, or category
groupbuys
See active group buys you can join
join <group_buy_id>
Join a group buy directly from WhatsApp
live
See active live shopping sessions
help
See all available commands
💡 How unified cart works: Add items on the web, then text cart to the bot — you'll see them all. Add items via WhatsApp, then open checkout on the web — they'll be there too. Same cart, any platform.
